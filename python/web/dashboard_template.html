What the data says (updates with your filters)

    Data notes & method

    Pipeline: raw CSV (180,519 lines × 53 columns) → Python cleaning (10 fixes, logged) → PostgreSQL star schema with 12 KPI views → Power BI and this web page.

    Scope: orders from 1 Jan 2015 to 31 Dec 2017. January 2018 (2,123 lines) is a partial month and is excluded.

    Late % is measured on shipped lines only. Cancelled and suspected-fraud orders never ship, so including them understates lateness (54.8% vs the correct 57.3%).

    Q4-2017 change: from October 2017 every order has one item and comes from a new customer. Full-year 2017 therefore shows sales −4.0% but Jan–Sep like-for-like shows +4.7%. Use the toggle above to compare fairly.

    Market × time: in most months one market carries over 95% of sales, so market totals partly reflect how many months each market appears.
